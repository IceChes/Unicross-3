# Unicross Corner Brackets
Unicross uses a variety of 90-degree angle corner brackets to secure various parts of the machine. The user is given the choice between large and small brackets.

There are a total of 12 corner brackets.
- 4 for the base. They support the vertical extrusions at the front of the machine.
- 4 for the sides. They support the Z axis side rails.
- 4 for the top. They brace the top of the printer.

Of these, the 8 for the base and the sides can be large or small. The top brackets **MUST** be large brackets, or you will risk the machine being pulled out of square, resulting in all kinds of artifacts.

The 4 for the base _should_ be large brackets, as this will help give stability to the entire machine. The back side of the printer is not as rigid as the front due to its construction, but further reinforcing the front will still help the back quite a lot.

The 4 for the side rails can be large or small. Large brackets will marginally improve rigidity, but small ones save you a couple bolts. 

TL;DR I recommend printing 8 large corner brackets and 4 small ones.
